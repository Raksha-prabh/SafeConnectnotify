About SafeConnect Notify

SafeConnect Notify is an AI-powered cybersecurity platform designed to identify suspicious social media accounts, detect fake profiles, and evaluate friend request safety.

Problem Statement

Millions of users face risks from fake accounts, phishing attempts, identity theft, and social engineering attacks on social media platforms.

Objectives

  • Detect fake profiles
  • Analyze account risk
  • Verify friend request safety
  • Provide security recommendations

Benefits

  • Improved user safety
  • Reduced scams
  • Better awareness
  • AI-based threat detection
